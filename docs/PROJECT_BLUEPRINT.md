# ROYALS INTELLIGENCE — COMPREHENSIVE PROJECT BLUEPRINT
**Target Entity:** Rajasthan Royals (RR)  
**Primary Analytical Season:** IPL 2026  
**Strategic Horizon:** IPL 2027 Auction Decision Support & Talent Acquisition  
**System Architecture:** Monorepo Distributed Sports Analytics Platform  

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Objective

ROYALS INTELLIGENCE is an elite, evidence-driven sports analytics and strategic decision support platform engineered exclusively for the Rajasthan Royals franchise. The platform is designed to transform raw cricket data (ball-by-ball, tracking telemetry, fitness metrics, venue conditions, and psychological scouting logs) into high-conviction strategic advantages.

The core mission is two-fold:
1. **In-Season Dominance (IPL 2026):** Deliver match-by-match pre-game tactical intelligence, pitch-adaptive playing XI & Impact Player optimization, live matchup advantage identification, and post-match analytical review.
2. **Auction & Roster Supremacy (IPL 2027 Auction):** Build an objective, valuation-backed recruitment engine that unearths hidden domestic/global talent, quantifies replacement-level upgrades, models auction dynamics, and maximizes purse efficiency.

## 2. Core Functional Modules (1 to 20)

### Module 01: IPL 2026 Season Intelligence
* **Scope:** Holistic macro-level monitoring of the IPL 2026 campaign.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Franchise performance trajectory tracking across league stages.
  * Net Run Rate (NRR) sensitivity modeling and qualification path simulations.
  * Phase-by-phase scoring rate and economy rate comparative benchmarking against league averages.
  * Points table projection engine using Monte Carlo simulations.

### Module 02: Match-by-Match Intelligence
* **Scope:** Granular tactical dossiers for individual fixture preparation.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Pre-game briefing packets detailing opponent weaknesses, powerplay tendencies, and death-over vulnerabilities.
  * Real-time phase leverage analysis (overs 1-6, 7-10, 11-15, 16-20).
  * Momentum shifts, Win Probability Added (WPA), and Expected Runs Added (ERA) tracking.

### Module 03: Stadium and Venue Intelligence
* **Scope:** Venue-specific dimensions, ground history, and environmental factors.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Boundary geometry profiling (off-side vs leg-side boundary lengths in meters, straight boundaries).
  * Altitude, air density, and travel fatigue impacts.
  * First-innings par score decay and chasing bias index (Day vs Night games).

### Module 04: Pitch and Weather Intelligence
* **Scope:** Micro-environmental analytics directly influencing ball behavior.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Soil composition tracking (black soil vs red soil moisture retention and grip coefficient).
  * Pitch deterioration curves (spin turn degrees and pace bounce variations as overs progress).
  * Real-time weather integration: dew point calculation, relative humidity impact on ball seam/grip, wind direction vectors affecting aerial boundaries.

### Module 05: Batting Intelligence
* **Scope:** Advanced batting diagnostics beyond traditional averages and strike rates.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Control percentage, false-shot frequency, and dot-ball mitigation index.
  * Entry-point performance: batsman strike rate acceleration profiles categorized by arrival over and wicket state.
  * Pace-vs-spin boundary frequency, wagon wheel distributions, and shot-type efficiency (pull, cut, sweep, ramp, loft).

### Module 06: Bowling Intelligence
* **Scope:** Deep-dive bowling diagnostics and release mechanics.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Release point consistency, release height, and trajectory variance tracking.
  * Length cluster analysis (full, good, back-of-a-length, short) mapped against batting strike zones.
  * Variation efficacy: slower balls (off-cutter, knuckle ball, back-of-hand) and yorker execution accuracy under high-pressure scenarios.

### Module 07: All-Rounder Intelligence
* **Scope:** Quantifying multi-dimensional utility and balance to team composition.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Dual-skill impact scoring combining batting expected value and bowling run prevention.
  * Phase bridging utility: players who stabilize middle-order collapses while providing 2+ overs of fifth-bowler relief.
  * Balance index: calculating the marginal win percentage gain provided by true 3D cricketers.

### Module 08: Uncapped Player Scouting
* **Scope:** Discovering emerging domestic Indian talents from state T20 leagues, Ranji Trophy, Vijay Hazare Trophy, and Syed Mushtaq Ali Trophy (SMAT).
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Cross-competition normalization: translating state-league numbers into IPL-calibrated expected metrics.
  * High-ceiling biomechanical indicators: genuine raw pace (>140 km/h) and clean ball-striking bat speeds.
  * Non-linear development curve tracking for players under 23 years old.

### Module 09: Capped Player Scouting
* **Scope:** Surveillance of established domestic and international cricketers.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Global franchise circuit monitoring (BBL, SA20, PSL, CPL, The Hundred, Vitality Blast).
  * Aging curves and injury recovery tracking.
  * Workload management and international bilateral release status.

### Module 10: Opposition Intelligence
* **Scope:** Systematic tactical deconstruction of all nine rival IPL franchises.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Captaincy and coaching decision trees under leverage points.
  * Opposition bowling change triggers and pinch-hitting patterns.
  * Bench depth vulnerability analysis when first-choice players are rotated or injured.

### Module 11: Player vs Player Matchups
* **Scope:** Head-to-head empirical and clustering-based micro-battles.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Historical ball-by-ball battle history (balls faced, dismissals, strike rate, dot ball percentage).
  * Archetype clustering: predicting outcomes when direct data is sparse (e.g., Left-arm orthodox bowling to right-handed aggressive openers).
  * Optimal bowler deployment matrix for incoming batters.

### Module 12: Batting Position and Bowling Role Intelligence
* **Scope:** Contextual role definition and position optimization.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Enforcer vs Anchor vs Finisher value attribution.
  * Powerplay strike bowler vs Middle-overs squeeze spinner vs Death-overs executioner.
  * Role fluidity index: evaluating player adaptability when shifted out of preferred batting slots.

### Module 13: Playing XI and Impact Player Optimisation
* **Scope:** Algorithmic team selection leveraging the IPL Impact Player rule.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Dynamic batting-first vs bowling-first substitution matrices.
  * Integer Linear Programming (ILP) optimizer to maximize expected team total under venue, opposition, and bowling quota constraints.
  * Contingency substitution trees in the event of top-order collapses or early bowler punishment.

### Module 14: Predictive Match Condition Engine
* **Scope:** Probabilistic outcome forecasting using Bayesian and Machine Learning architectures.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Real-time live win probability simulator incorporating dew factor and wicket leverage.
  * Innings score expectation models based on pitch decay and boundary dimensions.
  * Scenario tree modeling for captaincy tactical timeouts.

### Module 15: IPL 2027 Auction Intelligence
* **Scope:** Strategic command center for the IPL 2027 mega/mini auction.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Shadow auction simulations and dynamic price estimation models.
  * Rival purse exhaustion tracking and paddle-bidding threshold recommendations.
  * Roster construction optimizer: maximizing WAR (Wins Above Replacement) per INR crore allocated.

### Module 16: Fielding and Wicketkeeping Intelligence
* **Scope:** Quantifying defensive runs saved and spatial coverage.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Runs saved, catch conversion probability, and boundary-saving dive metrics.
  * Wicketkeeper reaction times, bye prevention, and stumping speed analytics.
  * Optimal field-placement generation based on batsman spray charts and bowler ball plans.

### Module 17: Partnership Analysis
* **Scope:** Synergies between batter pairings and bowling hunting pairs.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Strike rotation fluidity and running-between-the-wickets friction points.
  * Right-Left hand combination disruption quantification against opposition bowling captains.
  * Bowling hunting pairs: how economy at one end creates wicket opportunities at the other.

### Module 18: Player Form and Development Tracking
* **Scope:** Longitudinal tracking of skills, fitness, and mechanical consistency.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Exponential Moving Average (EMA) form indices that decay distant performances.
  * Technical flaw emergence alerts (e.g., widening gap between bat and pad against inward movement).
  * Strength & Conditioning correlation with bowling speed and agility.

### Module 19: Tactical Decision Review
* **Scope:** Post-match audit of captaincy, coaching, and analytical directives.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * DRS (Decision Review System) accuracy rating for team appeals.
  * Opportunity cost analysis of missed tactical bowling changes or promotion orders.
  * Post-match debrief automation with quantitative validation.

### Module 20: Data Reliability and Model Explainability
* **Scope:** Scientific integrity and trust architecture.
* **Key Capabilities:**
  * Sample size confidence intervals (Wilson score intervals, Bayesian priors) attached to every stat.
  * SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) values explaining model output drivers for coaching staff.
  * Transparent data lineage and confidence tagging (High, Moderate, Low Sample).

## 3. Advanced Tactical Capabilities (21 to 26)

### Module 21: Hidden Talent Discovery Engine
* **Objective:** Unearth undervalued diamonds in the rough using algorithmic anomaly detection across obscure domestic and youth scorecards.
* **Methodology:** Multi-factor talent clustering, age-adjusted efficiency ratios, and physical archetype matching.

### Module 22: Replacement and Upgrade Simulator
* **Objective:** In the event of player injury, national duty unavailability, or form slump, immediately simulate the exact marginal impact of replacements.
* **Methodology:** Monte Carlo roster simulation evaluating delta in expected season points and win percentages.

### Module 23: What-If Match Simulator
* **Objective:** Counterfactual tactical sandbox allowing analysts and coaches to replay matches with altered parameters.
* **Methodology:** Ball-by-ball Markov Chain transition models with parameterized bowler allocations and shot selections.

### Module 24: Form and Development Tracker
* **Objective:** Longitudinal monitoring of talent development and biomechanical resilience over multi-year horizons.
* **Methodology:** Bayesian state-space modeling tracking technical drift, bat swing velocity, and seam presentation stability.

### Module 25: Evidence and Confidence Engine
* **Objective:** Ensure coaches and leadership never make high-stakes auction decisions based on statistical noise.
* **Methodology:** Every metric displays confidence intervals, Bayesian credibility bands, and sample-size badges.

### Module 26: AI Scouting Report Generator
* **Objective:** Synthesize gigabytes of telemetry, historical statistics, video notes, and qualitative scout logs into executive tactical memos.
* **Methodology:** Structured LLM generation anchored to deterministic statistical pipelines, preventing hallucination through strict schema boundaries.
