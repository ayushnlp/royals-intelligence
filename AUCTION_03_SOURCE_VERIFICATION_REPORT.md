## 2. Reconciled Financial Framework: The ₹157 Crore Total Salary Cap

Under official BCCI regulations announced on September 28, 2024, the macro-financial framework for the 2025–2027 cycle establishes:

1. **Total Salary Cap ($\text{TSC}_{2027}$):**
   - **₹157.00 Crore** is the gross authorized franchise outlay ceiling for 2027.
   - It covers: (a) Auction Purse, (b) Match Fees, and (c) Incremental Performance Pay.
   - **It must NEVER be labelled as the auction purse.**
2. **Auction Purse ($P_{\text{auction}}$):**
   - Dedicated capital pool for auction room bidding and player retention contracts. (2025 base: ₹120.00 Cr; 2027 exact purse split is marked `NOT_YET_VERIFIED`).
3. **Match-Fee Expenditure ($E_{\text{match\_fees}}$):**
   - Official mandatory fee of **₹7.5 Lakhs per match** per playing member (Playing XI + Impact Player) = ₹0.90 Cr per match ($\approx \mathbf{\text{₹12.60 to ₹15.30 Crore}}$ over a 14 to 17 match season). Paid in addition to contracted salary.
4. **Remaining Available Auction Purse ($P_{\text{rem}}$):**
   - $$P_{\text{rem}} = P_{\text{auction}} - \sum S_{\text{retained}}$$
   - Direct subtraction of player contracts from ₹157.00 Crore is strictly barred ($P_{\text{rem}} \neq \text{₹157 Cr} - \sum S_{\text{contract}}$).
